# Vibeclubs Taste

Vibeclubs should feel like a late-night studio instrument: calm, precise, warm enough to invite action, and honest about every state.

## Keep

- Dark ink, amber timing signal, small green readiness cues.
- One dominant sentence per viewport.
- Quiet borders, generous breathing room, exact runtime labels.
- Product evidence that a visitor can operate.
- The five public words: vibeclub, host, crew, lock in, ship.

## Refuse

- Startup launch badges with stale dates.
- Fake social proof or simulated live activity.
- Generic AI gradients, glass-card mosaics, decorative globes, or unexplained WebGL.
- “Claude watches” language.
- Price cards for unverified plans.
- Excessive animation, parallax, cursor pursuit, or motion that competes with focus.
