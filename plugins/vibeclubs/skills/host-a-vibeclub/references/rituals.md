# Rituals that fit the clock

These are editable defaults, not rigid prescriptions. All times are elapsed minutes.

| Duration | Opening | Focus | Break | Final push | Share and close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 | 0–5 | 5–30 | 30–35 | 35–50 | 50–60 |
| 90 | 0–5 | 5–40 | 40–50 | 50–80 | 80–90 |
| 120 | 0–10 | 10–55 | 55–65 | 65–110 | 110–120 |

Opening: “What are you making? Pick a finish you can reach today.” Let people type or pass.
Focus: “Let's lock in. Questions go in chat; help is opt-in.”
Break: step away, stretch, refill water. Silence is welcome.
Final push: shrink the finish when needed; avoid introducing new scope.
Close: share one result or lesson. With a larger crew, use written wins and a few voluntary shares so closing fits.

## Craft adaptations

- Builders: choose one behavior, test, or fix. Finish with a demo, diff, or a clear blocker. Never expose credentials in screenshots.
- Writers: choose a scene, section, or revision. Finish with a short excerpt, word count supplied by the writer, or a private-work note.
- Music makers: choose a hook, arrangement passage, or mix decision. Headphones during focus; voluntary playback during closing with permission.
- Designers: choose one screen, interaction, or decision. Finish with a before/after or a named tradeoff.
- Mixed crew: share time and encouragement; keep individual outcomes independent.

## Contingencies

- Nobody else arrives: the host can complete a solo block and offer another date. Do not imply a crew attended.
- Someone arrives late: send the current phase and end time privately; let them join without restarting.
- Someone is stuck: offer a smaller next action or an optional pair during the break.
- Call or network fails: agree on a fallback contact before starting; continue locally. Do not claim clocks remained synchronized.
- Unfinished work: record what changed and the next action. Completion is not a condition of belonging.
