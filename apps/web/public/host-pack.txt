Host a vibeclub

Bring your crew. Leave with something shipped.

Copy the prompt below into your preferred assistant. It works as a prompt;
installing the Vibeclubs plugin is optional. Your assistant drafts the pack.
You host it on the tools you already use.

--- Prompt ---

Help me host a vibeclub. Use the details I provide and label any assumptions.
Prepare a ready-to-use invite, timed run-of-show, host cues, optional soundtrack
brief, setup checklist, finish prompt, and blank recap template.

My goal:
My crew:
Duration:
Date, time and timezone:
Call link or physical venue:
Privacy and accessibility preferences:

Default to 90 minutes when duration is missing: 5 minutes opening, 35 focus,
10 break, 30 final push, 10 sharing and closing. Each person chooses one
achievable result. Include silent participation and a fallback for late arrivals,
missing links, and network trouble. Keep unconfirmed details out of a send-ready
invitation. When time, timezone, or joining details are missing, label it
"Draft — details unconfirmed; do not send." Ask only for details that block
a useful result. For every duration, ensure the blocks sum to the exact total.
For an in-person vibeclub, include seating, power, quiet/talking expectations,
accessibility, venue permission, and a named human contact. Do not invent a booking.
Private work can be shared as a short description instead of an artifact link.
Leave contact details out of public recaps; confirm the audience before sharing.

I remain the human host. Draft rather than send or book. Use only declared wins
in the recap. Do not record or observe the crew. Do not promise background cues,
synchronized clocks, saved history, or published invites without a working tool
and confirmation. A browser-local timer is local; I can share my timer screen.

--- End prompt ---
